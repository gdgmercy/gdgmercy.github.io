What we do

Learn by building, together

Everything we run is hands-on and beginner-friendly. Show up, build something, and leave with a project you can point to.

Workshops

Guided, step-by-step sessions where you build something real, from a first website to your first AI app.

Hackathons

One-day build sprints like Mercy Hacks. Pick an idea, form a team, and ship it by the end of the day.

Building with Google AI

Hands-on time with Gemini, AI Studio, and Antigravity, so the tools feel familiar before you need them.

Community

A friendly group across two campuses. Ask questions, find teammates, and learn from each other on Discord.
