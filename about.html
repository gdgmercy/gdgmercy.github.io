About the chapter

Students who build together

We are the Google Developer Groups chapter at Mercy University, and our job is to help you go from curious to shipped.

Who we are

GDG on Campus Mercy University is a student-led chapter of Google’s GDG on Campus program, a worldwide network of university communities that learn about Google technologies by using them.

We are co-run with the Department of Mathematics & Computer Sciences, School of Liberal Arts, Mercy University, and we bring students together across the Dobbs Ferry and Manhattan campuses.

We are built for beginners. You do not need to be a computer science major, and you do not need to have written code before. Come with curiosity, and we will help you learn to build and ship real projects with Google technologies like Gemini and AI Studio.

Our mission

Help every Mercy student learn to build and ship real projects with Google technologies.

Learn

Short, guided workshops that explain the why, not just the clicks.

Build

Every session ends with something running on your own machine.

Ship
